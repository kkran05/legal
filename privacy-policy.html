Privacy Policy for Aria

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Aria is a menstrual cycle and health tracking app designed with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Important: Aria is a personal wellness and lifestyle tracking tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not make medical claims. Predictions and estimates are for informational and personal wellness purposes only. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical advice.

Why your health data stays private

Everything you log (periods, symptoms, moods, notes and all other health entries) is stored only on your phone, encrypted. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Aria uses only the outside services below, and none of them ever receives your health data.

Google Play and RevenueCat. They handle Aria+ purchases. Google Play processes the payment through your Google account, as for any app purchase. RevenueCat sees a random ID that is not linked to your name or email, what you bought, and basic device details such as the phone model and Android version. Like any internet connection, it also sees your phone's IP address. Why we use them: so you can buy Aria+ safely through Google Play, so we can confirm your purchase without an Aria account, and so you can get Aria+ back if you change phones.
Read the Google Play privacy policy · Read the RevenueCat privacy policy

Sentry (crash reports). Sentry receives crash reports, but only if you turn this on (it is off unless you choose it). A report contains technical details about the error and the device. Aria keeps your entries out of crash reports and removes health words from the reports it prepares. Why we use it (only if you turn it on): if the app crashes, a report tells us what went wrong so we can fix it in the next update. It helps make Aria more stable for everyone, but it is completely optional and the app works the same without it.
Read the Sentry privacy policy

Nothing about your health ever leaves your phone. If you leave crash reports off, the only thing that goes out is the Aria+ subscription check described above, which Aria makes even if you never buy Aria+.

1. Data We Collect and How It Is Stored

Health and Cycle Data (Stored Locally, Encrypted)

Your health entries are stored only on your device in an encrypted database (SQLCipher). This includes:

  • Period logs and cycle dates
  • Symptoms and mood entries
  • Basal body temperature (BBT) readings
  • Sleep quality and duration
  • Weight entries
  • Cervical mucus observations
  • Intimacy logs
  • Medication and supplement tracking
  • Exercise type, duration, and intensity
  • Water intake
  • Ovulation test (OPK) results
  • Notes and personal journal entries
  • Custom tags (user-created tracking tags with name, icon, color, and severity)
  • Cycle time capsules (personal messages to your future self with a target unlock date)
  • Cycle anomaly exclusions (cycles you have marked as anomalous, with optional cause tags such as stress, illness, or travel)
  • Pregnancy tracking data (due date, kick counter sessions, contraction timer sessions, pregnancy weight entries)
  • Postpartum data (lochia tracking, wellbeing check-in scores, delivery information, cycle return estimation)
  • Perimenopause data (stage estimation, hot flash tracking, wellness scores)
  • Kegel exercise sessions and streaks
  • Breast self-exam reminder settings
  • Contraceptive method (stored in your device's hardware-backed secure keystore)
  • Duress code / safety mode configuration (stored in your device's hardware-backed secure keystore, separate from your regular PIN)

Your health profile is stored encrypted in your device's secure keystore. Basic app settings (for example app mode, cycle and period length settings, cycle goal, and weight goal) are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and erased by Delete All Data.

Aria does not operate a server that receives or stores your health data. The developers of Aria cannot access your health data. Your health data stays on your device.

Subscription Management (RevenueCat SDK)

Aria uses the RevenueCat SDK to verify and manage optional Aria+ in-app subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat)
  • Purchase transaction data (subscription status, purchase timestamps)

This data is used solely to verify and manage your subscription. RevenueCat does not receive any of your health or cycle data. For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Crash Reports (Sentry SDK — Optional, Opt-In Only)

Aria includes an optional crash reporting feature powered by Sentry. This feature is disabled by default and only active if you explicitly opt in via Settings.

When enabled, Sentry may collect:

  • Crash logs and stack traces (technical error information)
  • Device metadata (OS version, app version, device model)

Sentry does not receive any of your health data, cycle information, notes, or any personal identifiers. You can disable crash reporting at any time in Settings. For more information, see Sentry's Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Aria explicitly does not:

  • Transmit your health or cycle data to any server
  • Use analytics or usage tracking services
  • Collect your name, email address, or other identifying information
  • Require you to create an account
  • Share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • Retain any server-side copy of your personal data

3. Data Security

Your data is protected at multiple layers:

  • Database encryption: All health data is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher, an industry-standard extension to SQLite
  • Secure keystore: Sensitive settings (contraceptive method, encryption keys, PIN hash) are stored in your device's hardware-backed secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain)
  • PIN lock: A numeric PIN to prevent unauthorized access to the app
  • Biometric lock: Fingerprint or face authentication (where supported by your device)
  • No auto-backup: Android auto-backup is disabled so health data is never automatically uploaded to Google's cloud backup service

We recommend enabling device encryption and app lock for maximum privacy.

Home Screen Widget (Android)

If you use the Android home screen widget, Aria stores your current cycle day and a brief status message in your device's SharedPreferences. This stays on your device, is cleared when you delete all data, and does not contain fertility-specific or sensitive language.

4. Your Rights and Data Control

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Export: Download your data in JSON or CSV format via Settings > Data Management
  • Local backup and import: Create an encrypted local backup and import supported Aria data on your device
  • Aria+ reports: Aria+ can generate PDF and provider-ready reports for discussion with your healthcare provider
  • Delete: Permanently erase all data from the app via Settings > Data Management > Delete All Data. This removes all health entries, encrypted settings, and widget cache
  • No account required: Aria never requires account creation, so there is no account to delete

5. GDPR Compliance

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the following applies:

Legal Basis for Processing

Health data is a special category of personal data under GDPR (Article 9). Aria processes your health data on the basis of your explicit consent, which you provide when you first use the app. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting all data.

All health-data processing described in this policy occurs on your device. Aria does not send that health data to a processor or developer-operated server.

Your GDPR Rights

  • Right of access: All your data is accessible directly within the app at all times
  • Right to rectification: You can edit or correct any entry within the app
  • Right to erasure: Use Settings > Data Management > Delete All Data
  • Right to data portability: Export all your data in JSON or CSV format
  • Right to withdraw consent: Delete all data and uninstall the app at any time
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You may contact your local data protection authority

6. Children's Privacy

Aria is only for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children or teenagers, and the onboarding age gate does not let anyone younger than 18 continue. Aria does not require a name, email address, or phone number. The optional patient name and date of birth you can type for a doctor report are only printed on that report and are not saved in your Aria data.
