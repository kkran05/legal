Aria+ Cancellation and Refund Help

Updated: July 12, 2026

Aria+ purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Use the same store account that completed the purchase.

Important: deleting or uninstalling Aria does not cancel a subscription.

Cancel on Google Play

  1. Open your subscriptions in Google Play.
  2. Select Aria.
  3. Choose Cancel subscription and follow the store instructions.

Official Google Play cancellation help

Cancel on Apple

Open the App Store subscription settings for your Apple Account, select Aria, and follow Apple's cancellation instructions.

Official Apple billing and subscription help

What Happens After Cancellation

Your store determines the effective cancellation date. Access normally continues through the period already confirmed by the store, and Aria's subscription screen shows the strongest verified access date available. Aria never extends access beyond a known expiry.

Request a Refund

Refund availability depends on the store's current policy, your purchase details, your location, and applicable law. Aria cannot promise that a refund will be granted.

  • Request a refund on Google Play
  • Google Play refund policies
  • Request a refund from Apple

Pending Purchases and Billing Issues

A pending purchase does not unlock Aria+ until the store confirms it. If the store reports a billing issue, Aria keeps access only through any verified paid period. Review your payment method in the store account or contact the store's support team.

Need More Help?

For an Aria app issue, contact ariatracker.support@gmail.com. For charges, eligibility, cancellation completion, or refund decisions, contact the store that processed the purchase.
